How to use

  • Start receiving connects the tuners. The beacon goes on tuner 1 whenever a tuner is spare.
  • Click a signal in the spectrum, or drag a numbered marker, to tune a tuner.
  • AutoTune gives detected stations to free tuners by itself.
  • Stream out sends each tuner's video to VLC, OBS or another PC on your network.
  • Record saves every transmission except the beacon into the Recordings folder, one folder per callsign.
  • The i and list buttons on each card show the signal and the transport stream details.

SettingsLNB and calibration
MHz

Enter the value printed on your LNB; many QO-100 LNBs use 9750 MHz. It is applied at once to all tuners and remembered for the next start.

Frequency calibration

Every tuner and LNB has a small frequency error. This measures it on the beacon (10491.500 MHz) and corrects it, separately for each tuner. It takes about a minute. The tuners are used for it, and go back to what they were doing afterwards.

Only the list of versions of this program is asked for. Nothing about you or your radio is sent.
